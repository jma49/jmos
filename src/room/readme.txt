The room

A desk at dusk, seen from its seat. It is a preview page of
majincheng.com: nothing links to it yet.

The room was modelled and lit in Blender, and its light is baked.
The browser draws it with three.js. The view outside the window is
a picture for each season, and it starts in the season of your own
date.

Three screens stand on the desk, and each can be pressed.

  left    a map of the United States, with the states visited
          marked; pressing one opens its photographs
  middle  this laptop: an editor holding a few of the page's own
          source files, and a terminal under it
  right   the site's desktop, running

In the terminal, help lists the commands. season winter turns the
view outside to winter; the other seasons go by their names too.

Escape, or a press beside a screen, goes back to the seat.
