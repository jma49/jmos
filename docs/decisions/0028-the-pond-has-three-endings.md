# 0028. The pond has three endings

- Date: 2026-11-15
- Status: accepted (the second ending is being built; the third is designed only)

## Context

The pond had ten looks, a seal for each, and small games beside them, but
nothing they led to: a visitor found a look and that was that. Jincheng
asked for one design over the whole pond in place of more separate games,
and then for a large puzzle with several endings of rising difficulty.

The story the puzzle tells is the pond's own. A painter learns by copying
(临): each of the ten looks is a master's pond that the gold koi has swum
in and learnt to paint. A copy is never signed. What a copyist is working
towards is the thing itself (真, as in 真迹, an original as against a
copy).

## Decision

Every visitor walks one way and stops where they like. Each stop is an
ending that stands by itself; nobody chooses a difficulty.

1. **The leap (跃).** Find the ten looks. The visitor's koi grows with
   each, and with all ten found its next leap goes as a dragon that winds
   down into the pond. Built. This is the ending everyone expects, and it
   is meant to feel like the end.
2. **The signature (款).** Each look has a place where a painting would be
   signed and this one is not. In a look, the gesture that found that look
   does nothing: once all ten are found, making it there lays one stroke
   in the empty place, in that look's own hand. Ten looks, ten strokes,
   and they are the ten strokes of 真 in the order it is written. With all
   ten laid, the character stands whole in the album and can be pressed:
   the gold koi swims over and round the visitor's, and the visitor has the
   seal 真, the first in the album that is not a master's.
3. **No name (无名).** Designed, not built: for whoever goes back after the
   second ending and finds the gold koi leaving the looks one by one.

How the second ending is kept fair:

- **Nothing new to learn.** A stroke is laid by a gesture the visitor
  already made once, to find that look. Where it is made does not matter:
  a phone has no room for aiming at a corner.
- **It can be stumbled on.** Holding still in ink is common, so the first
  stroke is often found by accident; that is the way in. The album then
  shows a leaf for the strokes, with the nine still wanted left open.
- **It is told, slowly.** After the dragon has gone down, the album's
  empty leaf is there for whoever opens it. A stroke not found by the
  second visit after that has a plainer line under its place.
- **Each stroke is a picture.** A stroke is painted for its look by the
  thread that paints the pond's pictures. In glow, the blueprint and ASCII
  it is light, ruled line and characters, which those looks are made of.
- **It is kept with the seals,** in this browser only.

## Consequences

- The dragon is no longer the last thing in the pond; the album says so
  only to a visitor who has seen it.
- The ten strokes are ten pictures to ask for, and 真 a seal to cut.
- A look that replaces another takes its stroke with it.
- The third ending depends on the solar terms and is its own decision when
  it is built.
